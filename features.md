# SMMly Features — SMM Panel, Reseller Tools and Business Workflows

SMMly is a social media marketing panel for creators, agencies, businesses and resellers. The platform is designed to bring social media services, customer orders and SMM business operations together.

This page describes the SMMly product areas and intended workflows. Actual availability depends on the features enabled and information published through the SMMly website.

## Social media marketing services

Discover supported social media marketing services through a central catalogue. Service information may include platform, description, pricing, minimum and maximum quantities, delivery notes and order requirements.

## SMM order management

Manage customer order workflows in one place, from placing an order to reviewing its progress. Order information and available actions depend on the selected service and current order state.

## Wallet and payment management

Review account balance and payment activity through the customer account. Available payment methods, funding instructions, transaction status and applicable terms are displayed by SMMly.

## Partial orders, refills, cancellations and refunds

SMM businesses need clear order lifecycle handling. SMMly provides customer guidance for partial order outcomes and applicable refill, cancellation and refund requests. Eligibility and outcomes depend on the service, order status and published terms.

## SMM reseller panel

SMMly is designed for agencies, digital service providers and resellers who manage social media services for their own customers. Reseller workflows can bring service discovery, customer orders and account operations into a central SMM platform.

## SMM API for developers

The SMMly API is intended to connect reseller panels, websites, applications, bots and server-side business tools. API documentation, authentication, supported actions and access instructions will be published through the official [SMMly API resources](https://github.com/smmly-co/smmpanel-api).

## Bulk and mass order workflows

Businesses handling many customer requests need efficient order operations. SMMly's product direction includes workflows for handling multiple orders, subject to the capabilities made available to each account.

## Referral and affiliate programme

Referral and partner-oriented growth capabilities form part of the SMMly ecosystem. Programme availability, eligibility, tracking, rewards and terms are governed by the information published by SMMly.

## Telegram and automation integrations

SMMly's integration ecosystem is intended to help businesses connect their workflows with supported tools and automations. Integration availability and setup instructions will be published through official SMMly resources.
