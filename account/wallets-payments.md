# Wallets and payments

SMMly uses a prepaid account balance for eligible orders.

> **Availability note:** Payment methods, fees, currencies, and top-up rules depend on the configuration shown in the customer's Add funds flow.

## Adding funds

1. Open Add funds.
2. Choose an available payment method.
3. Review the amount, currency, fees, and any applicable bonus before continuing.
4. Complete the payment with the payment provider.
5. Wait for the payment confirmation and updated account balance.

A payment being initiated is not the same as being confirmed. SMMly should credit an account only after the payment has been verified through the relevant payment flow.

## Balance and transaction history

The account balance is used to pay for SMMly orders. The transaction history should make top-ups, order charges, and order-related credits traceable.

The balance displayed in the account and the API must be derived from the same authoritative financial records.

## Partial and canceled orders

Eligible unused order value is credited back to the SMMly balance after the final order outcome and refund amount have been verified. The resulting credit should appear in the transaction history.

## Withdrawals

Do not assume that panel balance can be withdrawn as cash. The applicable account and refund policy defines how unused funds are handled.

## Payment support

For a missing or delayed top-up, contact support with the payment reference and payment method. Do not share full card numbers, security codes, passwords, or API keys.
