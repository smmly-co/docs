# SMMly — Social Media Marketing Panel

## Your SMM business, in one panel.

SMMly is a social media marketing (SMM) panel for creators, agencies, businesses and resellers. It is designed to bring service discovery, order operations, account activity and reseller workflows together.

Whether you manage social media services for your own projects or operate a customer-facing SMM business, SMMly provides a central place to explore the platform and its supported workflows.

## SMMly platform features

### Social media services catalogue
Explore supported social media marketing services and review the service information, pricing and order requirements presented in the product.

### SMM order management
Place orders through the available customer workflows and follow order progress from your account.

### Account balance and payments
Review account balance and payment activity. Available funding methods and payment terms are shown through the product.

### Order lifecycle support
SMMly documentation covers customer questions around order progress and applicable order support, including partial orders, refills, cancellations and refunds where offered for an order.

### SMM reseller platform
SMMly is designed for agencies, operators and resellers who manage social media marketing services for customers. Reseller workflows bring service discovery and order operations into a central SMM platform.

### SMM API and developer integrations
SMMly developer resources are intended for businesses connecting their own websites, reseller panels, applications, bots and server-side automations. See the [SMMly API repository](https://github.com/smmly-co/smmpanel-api) for official documentation and access information as it is published.

### Referrals and partner growth
Referral and partner-oriented capabilities form part of the SMMly product ecosystem. Current programme availability, eligibility and terms are communicated through SMMly.

### Business workflows and automation
SMMly is designed to support operators who need organised order workflows and integrations across their SMM business. Specific automation and bulk-operation capabilities depend on the product features made available to an account.

## Availability

Feature access, supported services, payment options, programme terms and integration availability can change. The SMMly website and signed-in product experience are the source of truth for current availability.

Visit [smmly.co](https://smmly.co) to explore SMMly.
