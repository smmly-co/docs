# SMMly — Frequently asked questions

## What is SMMly?

SMMly is a social media marketing (SMM) panel for creators, agencies, businesses and resellers. It is designed to bring social media services, order management, account activity and business workflows into one platform.

## Who is SMMly for?

SMMly is designed for social media marketers, agencies, digital service providers, SMM panel operators and resellers.

## What services are available?

Supported platforms, services, rates, limits and order requirements are shown in the SMMly service catalogue. Availability can vary by service.

## How do I place an order?

Sign in to your SMMly account, choose an available service and follow the order instructions shown for that service.

## Where can I track an order?

Use the order area in your SMMly account to review available order information and progress.

## What does a partial order mean?

An order may be marked partial when it is not completed in full. Any applicable balance adjustment or next step is governed by the order details and SMMly's published terms.

## Are refills or cancellations available?

Eligibility depends on the service and order status. Follow the options and terms displayed for the relevant order in SMMly.

## How do payments and account balance work?

Use the account and payment options shown after signing in. Available payment methods, funding instructions and terms are displayed in the product.

## Does SMMly support resellers?

SMMly is designed for agencies, operators and resellers managing social media marketing services for customers. Reseller workflow and integration availability is communicated through SMMly's official channels.

## Does SMMly provide an API?

SMMly is preparing developer resources for businesses that want to connect websites, reseller panels, applications and automations. Follow the [SMMly API repository](https://github.com/smmly-co/smmpanel-api) for official documentation and access information.

## Does SMMly support referrals or affiliates?

Referral and partner-oriented capabilities are part of the SMMly product ecosystem. Current availability, eligibility and reward terms are communicated by SMMly.

## How do I get support?

Use the support options published on [smmly.co](https://smmly.co). Include the relevant order or payment reference when asking for help. Never share passwords or payment security details.

## Where can I find SMMly documentation?

Visit the [SMMly documentation repository](https://github.com/smmly-co/docs) for product information and guides.
